# Home page (/)

Scope: the agency home page, Persuade mode, Arabic RTL first. Audience: Riyadh business owners (cafés and F&B first) arriving from social media on a phone. Action: book a meeting on WhatsApp. Proof on hand: services, two real packages, brand assets. Clients come later as labelled slots, never invented. Constraints: binding brand guidelines (see PRODUCT.md).

Build path: code-led (no image generation used; the user was not asked about the comp path).

## Direction contract

THESIS: "WATCH US" played straight. Mainstream is the agency that watches. The page is a case file the eye opens on your business. It refuses the agency default of a split hero, logo strip, and three equal service cards.

OWN-WORLD: dark beige (#B3A081) dossier stock as the ground. Ink black frames, halftone dot grids on Mainstream Red, white case cards with a hard black offset shadow, red and black ribbons tilted 2–4°, and huge ghost words in Gloock. Rakkas for Arabic headlines, Tajawal for body, Space Grotesk Bold for tracked labels. The eye with its wave is the reticle; a red tilted stamp is the verdict.

STORY: the visitor learns that Mainstream is a whole marketing team, nine roles, for one employee's salary. They see what the team does, then a real package with a real price, and come to believe it is concrete and local. They act by opening WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Rakkas headline "فريقك التسويقي المتكامل" top-right at about 7–9vw. Below it a 3×3 surveillance wall of halftone frames, each holding an eye and a role label. The eye reticle sweeps across the frames, each role typing in as it is passed, then the red stamp "براتب موظف واحد" lands tilted over the wall. The WhatsApp CTA is a red ribbon button under the headline. A ghost "WATCH" sits behind everything, with the nav strip on top.

FORM: spy-dossier title sequence (catalog challenger, chosen by the user), fused with the brand's own system. seed:47c66223b7e4d6f9

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: reticle sweep, typed roles and the stamp landing in the hero. Further down, case files open in place (services), and the package is a stamped dossier with a price tag.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
