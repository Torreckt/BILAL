# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js / React (user's choice). Node.js LTS installed for it on 2026-10-21.

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-size businesses in Riyadh and the wider Saudi market, especially cafés and F&B, who need marketing done for them and have no in-house marketing team. They arrive from social media or a referral, mostly on a phone, and are deciding whether Mainstream is worth a meeting.

## Product Purpose

Mainstream is a Riyadh marketing agency. The site exists to turn a curious visitor into a booked meeting over WhatsApp. Success = a WhatsApp conversation started.

## Positioning

"Your complete marketing team for the salary of one employee": a full team (strategist, content writer, designer, photographer, editor, social manager, campaign manager, influencer marketer, performance analyst) for one monthly cost. Vertical packages go beyond marketing: the café package bundles marketing with barista training, food-safety readiness and brand experience.

## Operating Context

Saudi market, Arabic-first (RTL), Saudi conversational voice. Channels the agency runs: Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Google Maps reviews, influencers. Primary conversion channel: WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: social media management, product photography and Reels, paid campaigns (Instagram / TikTok / Snapchat), influencer collaborations, Google Maps review management, menu design, visual identity and packaging, seasonal offers and activations, monthly performance reports.
- Café package ("باقة الكوفيهات"): 4,000 SAR. Includes marketing and content, barista training (two days a month with trainer Bilal: coffee types and origins, grind levels, extraction ratio, KPIs), food safety (hygiene and handling, storage and temperatures, cross-contamination, municipal inspection readiness), identity and experience, and 3 paid ads (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat). Agency gift: one extra free week in the first month for every new subscriber.
- "Your complete marketing team" offer: shown without a published price.
- Prices are shown on the site (user confirmed).
- Open: WhatsApp number, social handles and email are not yet provided; use clearly marked placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

Binding brand guidelines (Mainstream-Brand-Guidelines.pdf):
- Name: Mainstream / ماين ستريم. Taglines: "We create the hype. You own the spotlight." / "نصنع الضجة.. وأنت تملك الأضواء". Sign-off: "انت استرخ.. وخلي الإبداع علينا". "WATCH US".
- Logo: the eye (attention, spotlight) plus the wave (the stream of content and hype). The logo is used only on dark beige, black or white. Never recolour the eye (white, red iris, black pupil), never stretch, tilt or add shadows to the logo, and keep clear space at least one eye-width.
- Colours: Dark Beige #B3A081, Mainstream Red #A3121F, Bright Red #E0303B, Ink Black #111111, Off-White #F2EDE4, White #FFFFFF.
- Type: Gloock (English display), Rakkas (Arabic display), Space Grotesk Bold (tracked labels and ribbons), Tajawal (Arabic body: Regular, Medium, Bold, ExtraBold).
- Visual elements: the eye, the wave (divider), tilted ribbons (red or black, tilted 2–4°), hard offset black shadow with no blur, halftone (black dots on red, poster style), and huge semi-transparent ghost words behind the design.
- Assets: Primary logo, Arabic logo and Dark logo PNGs; café package and "complete team" marketing visuals (in the user's brand folder).

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: services list, café package contents and price, team roles, taglines, brand assets.
- The agency has real clients, but the user will add them later. Never fabricate client names, logos, testimonials, numbers or results; leave labelled slots instead.

## Product Principles

1. Every path ends at WhatsApp.
2. Show the whole team, not a vendor: the "one salary" mechanism is the hook.
3. Speak Saudi, confident and playful, never corporate.
4. Claims stay true: no invented clients or metrics.
